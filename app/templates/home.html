How it works

  1. Paste a link

    Copy a song link from Spotify or YouTube and paste it above.

  2. We analyse the song

    We look up the song's details, such as genre, mood and artist, using official music services.

  3. Get recommendations

    We find songs listeners connect with it, rank them and explain why each one might appeal to you.

  4. Keep exploring

    Open a song on its platform, or use any recommendation as the starting point for a new search.

Supported links look like https://open.spotify.com/track/…, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=… or https://youtu.be/….
